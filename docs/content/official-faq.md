## 1. General

### Where is Nyalian Tourism Village located?
Nyalian Village is located in Banjarangkan District, Klungkung Regency, Bali — not Bangli Regency as earlier project material incorrectly stated (see the correction note in `official-tourism-content.md`). The nearest well-known landmark is the Klungkung Palace / Kertha Gosa complex in Semarapura, Klungkung's regency capital.

### What makes Nyalian different from other tourism villages in Bali?
Nyalian combines two identities that are rare to find together: it is a living royal heritage site (Puri Agung Nyalian, birthplace of Arja Gede Garong and home of the sacred Tari Baris Tentara) and a working agrarian village where rice farming and animal husbandry remain the primary livelihood. Visitors experience traditions that are still actively practiced by the community that owns them, not staged re-creations.

### Is Nyalian a large or heavily commercialized tourist destination?
No. Nyalian's tourism model is community-based and heritage-led, developed with support from the Klungkung Tourism Office (Dinas Pariwisata Klungkung) and the Klungkung Tourism Village Communication Forum (Forkomdewi Klungkung). It is intentionally not positioned as a mass-tourism site.

### Is Nyalian suitable for families?
Most experiences are suitable for families, including the Heritage Morning, Melukat Experience, and Village Life & Craft Immersion packages. The Tukad Melangit Trekking & Tirta Harum package involves uneven forest and ravine terrain and is better suited to active travelers comfortable with walking. **[Confirm with Pokdarwis Nyalian]** any age or fitness restrictions before publishing family-specific guidance.

### Do local guides speak English?
**[Confirm with Pokdarwis Nyalian]** — not yet documented. (Note: a 2019 community training program for Dusun Pemenang's capil artisans did include basic English instruction aimed at tourism readiness, per `official-umkm.md`, but this does not confirm guide-level language capability across the wider village.)

## 2. Location & Getting There

### How do I get to Nyalian from Denpasar or the airport?
General village-level directions from Denpasar or Ngurah Rai International Airport are **[Confirm with village authorities]** — not yet documented. What is documented so far is route-specific:
- **Pura Tirta Tadah Uwug:** from central Semarapura, the route runs through Banjarangkan District toward Nyalian, using Pura Puseh Umanyar as a landmark. A digital map search for "Tirta Tadah Uwuk Desa Nyalian" is also effective.

## 3. Destinations

### What destinations can I visit in Nyalian?
Nyalian currently documents five destinations:

| Destination | Category | Signature Experience |
|---|---|---|
| Puri Agung Nyalian | Cultural & Heritage Tourism | Royal residence, birthplace of Arja, home of the sacred Baris Tentara dance |
| Pura Tirta Tadah Uwug | Spiritual Tourism | Melukat (purification bathing) at a cliffside sacred spring |
| Pura Kahyangan Desa Nyalian | Spiritual Tourism / Performing Arts | Site of the Sang Hyang Jaran Mesiram trance ritual |
| Pura Tirta Harum | Nature & Agriculture / Spiritual Tourism | Hidden clan-origin forest temple, reachable only on foot |
| Tukad Melangit Valley & Rice Field Landscape | Nature & Agriculture | Riverine valley and working rice-terrace scenery |

### Can I just show up at Puri Agung Nyalian, or do I need to arrange a visit in advance?
Puri Agung Nyalian is an inhabited royal residence and active ceremonial site, not a museum with standard opening hours. Visits should be arranged in advance through the village tourism group (Pokdarwis) or directly with the puri, out of respect for its dual status as a home and a living cultural institution.

### What is melukat, and can anyone take part?
Melukat is a spiritual purification bathing ritual. At Pura Tirta Tadah Uwug, 9 of the site's 10 sacred spouts (pancoran) are available for general purification use (the 10th is reserved for pitra yadnya funerary rites). General visitors are welcomed for guided melukat as part of the Sacred Waters Melukat Experience package, led by a local pemangku or trained guide.

### Can I visit Pura Tirta Harum on my own, without a guide?
Pura Tirta Harum is reachable only on foot, via forest trekking from either Desa Tamanbali or Desa Nyalian, along the Tukad Melangit ravine. As a kawitan (clan-origin) temple, its general-visitor access policy — for those outside the Mahagotra Tirta Harum lineage — is **[Confirm with the temple's pengempon/caretakers]**. Until confirmed, a guide-led visit (as offered in Tour Package 4) is the recommended way to experience the site respectfully.

### What are the opening hours and entrance fees for these destinations?
**[Confirm with village authorities]** — opening hours, entrance fees, and whether a local guide is required are not yet publicly documented for any of the five destinations. This FAQ will be updated once Pokdarwis Nyalian confirms these details.

### Where can I see the Sang Hyang Jaran Mesiram ritual?
At a Kahyangan temple within Desa Nyalian — most likely one of Desa Pakraman Nyalian's Kahyangan Tiga temples, though the exact temple is still **[Confirm with village adat authorities]**. See the "Sacred Sites & Etiquette" section below for how (and whether) visitors may witness it.

## 4. Tour Packages & Booking

### What tour packages are available?

### Are these prices final?
No. Every price above is a placeholder estimate for prototyping purposes, based on comparable community-based tourism packages elsewhere in Bali. None are confirmed. Final pricing will be set by Pokdarwis Nyalian and, where relevant, Puri Agung Nyalian or participating host families.

### How do I book a tour package?
**[Confirm with Pokdarwis Nyalian]** — an official booking channel (phone number, WhatsApp, or email) has not yet been documented. This answer will be updated once contact information is finalized in a future revision.

### Do prices go down for groups?
Prices are typically lower per person for groups of 4 or more. Exact group and private-guide pricing structures are still **[Confirm with Pokdarwis Nyalian]**.

### Is transportation to Nyalian included in the packages?
No. All seven packages explicitly exclude transportation to/from Nyalian, except that Package 3 (Nyalian Full-Day Discovery) notes transportation could potentially be bundled as a hotel-pickup add-on — this add-on is not yet confirmed.

### Can I book the Sacred Ceremony Witness package (Package 6) for a specific date?
No — and this is intentional. Package 6 is not a scheduled show. It is a standing, by-request arrangement tied entirely to the temple/palace's own ceremonial calendar (the Napak Pertiwi procession or a Piodalan at Pura Kahyangan Desa Nyalian). Advance registration is recommended 2–4 weeks ahead so the village/puri can confirm whether an upcoming ceremony is appropriate for visitor observation. The package cannot guarantee that any ceremony will occur during a given stay.

### Can I stay overnight in Nyalian?
Yes, through Package 7 (Nyalian Immersive Stay — 2 Days 1 Night), which includes one night of homestay accommodation with a local family. Homestay family participation and capacity are still **[Confirm with participating host families]**.

## 5. Sacred Sites & Etiquette

### Can I watch Tari Baris Tentara or Sang Hyang Jaran Mesiram whenever I visit?
No. Both are sacred, restricted (*pingit*) rituals tied to their own ceremonial calendars — Tari Baris Tentara during the Napak Pertiwi procession at Puri Agung Nyalian, and Sang Hyang Jaran Mesiram during Piodalan at a Kahyangan temple within the village. Neither is ever staged on demand for tourists. The only way to potentially witness either is through Package 6 (Sacred Ceremony Witness), and only if travel dates happen to align with an actual ceremony and community/temple permission allows.

### Can I take photos or video of these sacred rituals?
Photography and video permission must be confirmed case-by-case for each specific occasion — never assume it is allowed. Treat photography as prohibited by default unless a guide or community representative specifically confirms otherwise for that occasion. Do not use imagery from other villages' Sanghyang or Baris performances as a substitute.

### What should I wear or how should I behave for melukat at Pura Tirta Tadah Uwug?
General Balinese temple etiquette (modest dress, sarong/kamen) should be expected; the Sacred Waters Melukat Experience package includes sarong/kamen rental if needed. Specific dress-code or conduct guidelines beyond this are **[Confirm with Desa Adat Umanyar / site management]**.

### Is Pura Tirta Harum open to visitors who aren't part of the Mahagotra Tirta Harum lineage?
**[Confirm with the temple's pengempon/caretakers]** — not yet confirmed. This materially affects how the destination can be marketed and is treated as an open question in `official-destinations.md`.

### Why does this project treat some traditions as "bookable" and others as "sacred and restricted"?
Nyalian's cultural heritage falls into two categories, and this documentation set treats them differently on purpose: Arja Gede Garong is performing-art/entertainment heritage and can be responsibly scheduled as a visitor experience (as in Package 1). Tari Baris Tentara and Sang Hyang Jaran Mesiram are sacred ritual heritage — the Nyalian community itself classifies Baris Tentara as *sesolahan pingit* (restricted sacred performance) — and are never presented as bookable. This distinction protects both cultural accuracy and the community's trust in the tourism program.

## 6. Facilities & Practical Info

### Are there restrooms and parking at the destinations?
Confirmed at Pura Tirta Tadah Uwug: a parking area, a stone stairway down to the bathing complex, public toilets, and changing rooms/areas. Facilities at the other four destinations are **[Confirm with village authorities]** — none have been publicly documented yet, and Pura Agung Nyalian and Pura Tirta Harum in particular should not be assumed to have standard visitor infrastructure.

### Is there anywhere to eat nearby?
Yes — Tadah Uwuk Coffee and Eatery, located on-site at Pura Tirta Tadah Uwug, serves coffee, juices, and traditional Balinese food. Several tour packages (Nyalian Full-Day Discovery, Nyalian Immersive Stay) also include meals with a local homestay family.

### What should I bring for the Tukad Melangit trekking route?
Package 4 (Tukad Melangit Trekking & Tirta Harum) includes a trail snack and drinking water. Visitors should bring their own suitable trekking shoes/gear, as this is explicitly excluded from the package price, and the route involves uneven forest and ravine terrain.

## 7. Local Crafts (UMKM)

### Can I buy handmade souvenirs in Nyalian?
Yes. Capil — a traditional wide-brimmed hat woven from young coconut leaf (janur) — is a hereditary craft concentrated in Dusun Pemenang. It is Nyalian's most documented local craft and is featured directly in the tourism program.

### Can I meet the artisans and see the hats being made?
Yes, through the Village Life & Craft Immersion package (Package 5) and Day 2 of the Nyalian Immersive Stay (Package 7). Both include an artisan-led capil-weaving demonstration, a hands-on mini-workshop, and a take-home capil hat. A current, complete roster of artisans willing to be named publicly is still **[Confirm with Desa Adat Pemenang / Pokdarwis Nyalian]**, pending their consent.

### Does the workshop fee directly benefit the artisans?
That is the intent, and it is treated as a design requirement in `official-umkm.md`: workshop pricing is meant to be set separately from ordinary wholesale hat pricing (historically Rp 15,000–25,000 per hat) and should ensure fair, direct payment to the participating artisan(s). The exact fee-sharing structure is still **[Confirm with Pokdarwis Nyalian]**.
