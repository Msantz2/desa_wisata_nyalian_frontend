## 1. Nyalian Heritage Morning

**Theme:** Cultural & Heritage Tourism
**Included Destinations:** Puri Agung Nyalian
**Duration:** Half-day, approx. 4 hours (e.g., 08:00–12:00)
**Target Visitors:** Culture-focused travelers, history enthusiasts, small groups, students/educational groups
**Group Size:** 2–15 people

### Itinerary / Activities
- Welcome and orientation at Puri Agung Nyalian's outer courtyard
- Guided walk through the puri's historic architecture and courtyards
- Storytelling session: the founding of Nyalian and the Puri's role as center of the historic West Klungkung Kingdom
- **Arja Gede Garong cultural performance excerpt** — a short, scheduled demonstration of Nyalian's signature folk theatre tradition, performed by local Arja practitioners (not a sacred rite — safe to present as a regular bookable show)
- Q&A with a local cultural guide
- Light traditional refreshment (Balinese snacks and coffee/tea)

### Highlights
- Only place in Bali to learn the origin story of Arja directly at its birthplace
- Access to a still-inhabited royal residence, not a static museum
- Small-group, guide-led storytelling format

### Estimated Price
**Rp 175,000 / person** — includes guide, entrance/courtesy contribution to Puri Agung Nyalian, Arja excerpt performance, and refreshments. *(Placeholder — confirm with Puri Agung Nyalian and Pokdarwis Nyalian.)*

### Inclusions / Exclusions
- ✅ Local guide, Arja performance excerpt, refreshments, courtesy contribution to Puri
- ❌ Transportation to/from Nyalian, personal expenses

## 2. Sacred Waters Melukat Experience

**Theme:** Spiritual Tourism
**Included Destinations:** Pura Tirta Tadah Uwug
**Duration:** Half-day, approx. 3 hours (e.g., 07:00–10:00, recommended morning slot)
**Target Visitors:** Wellness travelers, spiritual tourists, individuals and couples seeking a quieter, reflective experience
**Group Size:** 1–10 people

### Itinerary / Activities
- Meet at Pura Tirta Tadah Uwug parking area, brief orientation on melukat etiquette and the site's spiritual meaning
- Descend the stone stairway to the bathing complex
- Guided melukat (purification bathing) ritual at the designated spouts (pancoran), led by a local pemangku or trained guide
- Quiet reflection time by the river
- Post-ritual rest and refreshment at Tadah Uwuk Coffee and Eatery

### Highlights
- A genuinely sacred, still-active spring — not a constructed spa experience
- Small-group and private options available for a more personal, contemplative pace
- Includes context on the symbolic meaning of the site's 10 pancoran

### Estimated Price
**Rp 150,000 / person** — includes guide/ritual facilitation, offering materials (canang/sarana), and one beverage at Tadah Uwuk Coffee and Eatery. *(Placeholder — confirm with Desa Adat Umanyar and site management.)*

### Inclusions / Exclusions
- ✅ Guide, basic offering materials, one beverage, sarong/kamen rental if needed
- ❌ Transportation, additional food, personal donations

## 4. Tukad Melangit Trekking & Tirta Harum

**Theme:** Nature & Agriculture / Spiritual Tourism
**Included Destinations:** Tukad Melangit Valley & Rice Field Landscape, Pura Tirta Harum
**Duration:** Half-day, approx. 4–5 hours (e.g., 07:00–11:30/12:00, recommended early start)
**Target Visitors:** Active travelers, trekkers, nature photographers, visitors comfortable with uneven forest/ravine terrain
**Group Size:** 2–10 people (smaller groups recommended for trail conditions)

### Itinerary / Activities
- Meet at the Nyalian-side trailhead, safety and etiquette briefing
- Trek along the Tukad Melangit ravine through forest terrain toward Pura Tirta Harum
- Arrival at Pura Tirta Harum: guided explanation of the site's role as a kawitan (clan-origin) temple for the Mahagotra Tirta Harum lineage, and respectful outer-area visitation (no ritual participation unless visitor is of the lineage)
- Return trek, with stops at scenic ravine viewpoints
- Light trail snack and water provided

### Highlights
- Bali's most under-the-radar forest-temple trek — not on standard tourist circuits
- Genuine physical activity paired with cultural depth
- Small-group, guide-required format preserves the site's quiet character

### Estimated Price
**Rp 225,000 / person** — includes trekking guide, trail snack/water, and courtesy contribution at Pura Tirta Harum. *(Placeholder — general-visitor access policy for Pura Tirta Harum should be confirmed with the temple's pengempon/caretakers before this package is finalized; see `official-destinations.md` confirmation list.)*

### Inclusions / Exclusions
- ✅ Trekking guide, trail snack, drinking water, courtesy contribution
- ❌ Trekking shoes/gear, transportation to trailhead, personal insurance

## 6. Sacred Ceremony Witness (Seasonal, By Request)

**Theme:** Performing Arts / Spiritual Tourism — **Exclusive, non-guaranteed experience**
**Included Destinations:** Puri Agung Nyalian (Pamerajan Agung) and/or Pura Kahyangan Desa Nyalian, depending on which ceremony aligns with the visit
**Duration:** Varies — tied entirely to the temple/palace's own ceremonial calendar
**Target Visitors:** Repeat visitors, cultural researchers, travelers specifically seeking rare/authentic ritual access, and travelers whose visit happens to coincide with a ceremonial date
**Group Size:** Small groups strongly preferred (5–8 max), out of respect for the ceremony

### What This Package Actually Offers
This is **not a scheduled show**. It is a standing arrangement: if a visitor's travel dates happen to align with either the **Napak Pertiwi** procession (when Tari Baris Tentara is performed at the Pamerajan Agung) or a **Piodalan** at Pura Kahyangan Desa Nyalian (when Sang Hyang Jaran Mesiram may occur), and community/temple permission allows, registered visitors on this package may be given the opportunity to respectfully observe from an appropriate distance, with a guide who can explain the ritual's meaning in real time.

Because both events are sacred, trance-based, and pingit (restricted) in nature, **photography/video may be limited or prohibited** depending on the occasion, and the package cannot guarantee that any ceremony will occur during a given stay.

### How It Should Be Sold
- List this package as "date availability on request" rather than a fixed-date bookable item
- Require advance registration (recommend minimum 2–4 weeks) so the village/puri can confirm whether an upcoming ceremony is appropriate for visitor observation
- Be transparent in all marketing copy that this is a rare-access, weather-permitting-style offer — the honesty is itself part of the appeal for the serious cultural traveler

### Highlights
- Access to two of Bali's most rarely-witnessed sacred rituals, framed with full respect for their religious context
- A guide-led explanation that helps visitors understand what they're witnessing, not just watch it
- Directly reinforces Nyalian's tourism philosophy of "living culture over performance" as a marketing strength, not a limitation

### Estimated Price
**Rp 300,000 / person, when a ceremony date is confirmed available** — includes guide, any required courtesy/ceremonial contribution, and coordination with temple/puri authorities. *(Placeholder — actual pricing, minimum notice period, and whether outside visitors may attend at all must be confirmed directly with Puri Agung Nyalian and the relevant desa pakraman before this package is published or sold.)*

### Inclusions / Exclusions
- ✅ Guide/cultural interpreter, coordination with temple/puri, ceremonial courtesy contribution
- ❌ Guaranteed ceremony occurrence, photography rights (case-by-case), transportation

## 7. Nyalian Immersive Stay — 2 Days 1 Night

**Theme:** Full Immersion (combines all pillars)
**Included Destinations:** Puri Agung Nyalian, Pura Tirta Tadah Uwug, Tukad Melangit Valley & Rice Field Landscape, Village Life & Craft (UMKM)
**Duration:** 2 Days, 1 Night
**Target Visitors:** Travelers seeking deep immersion, honeymooners wanting an authentic detour from Bali's beach areas, small groups, slow-travel enthusiasts
**Group Size:** 2–10 people

- Arrival and check-in at a local homestay
- Afternoon: Puri Agung Nyalian heritage walk and Arja excerpt (as Package 1)
- Evening: home-cooked family dinner and informal storytelling with the host family

- Mid-morning: rice-terrace and valley walk

### Highlights
- The only package that includes an overnight homestay, generating direct income for a village family
- Covers all four Visitor Experience Pillars from `official-tourism-content.md` in a single itinerary
- Best option for visitors who want to experience village life, not just tour it

### Estimated Price
**Rp 950,000 / person** (based on double-occupancy homestay) — includes one night homestay accommodation, all meals, all guiding, melukat facilitation, and the craft workshop. *(Placeholder — homestay rates and availability to be confirmed with participating host families.)*
